Saliency

|∂‖emb‖ / ∂pixel|

Gradient of the embedding's length with respect to each pixel (largest over the colour channels), scaled from least to most important pixel: the stronger the tint, the more that pixel moves the embedding. Desktop runs with a ViT-tiny encoder add a third map: how much the last block's [CLS] token attends to each patch, averaged over heads.

Evaluate with a few labels

Freeze the encoder, fit a linear probe and a k-NN classifier on a small labeled subset, and score a held-out split.

Nothing to evaluate yet. Train a model first, then score it here with a few labels.

Probe settings

10%

20% of the images are held out for scoring; the labeled subset comes from the rest.

  • Linear probe: softmax regression on standardized features, full-batch Adam, 100 epochs.
  • k-NN: cosine similarity, k = 20, majority vote, ties go to the nearest neighbour.
  • Random-init: the same MLP, untrained.
  • Supervised: the same MLP trained with cross-entropy on the same labeled subset.
